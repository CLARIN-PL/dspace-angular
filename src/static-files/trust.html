Trust and transparency

How we maintain a trustworthy repository

CLARIN-PL combines institutional responsibility, expert curation, interoperable metadata, controlled access and long-term preservation.

CoreTrustSeal document status

The repository was awarded a CoreTrustSeal assessment on 17 December 2019 under the Core Trustworthy Data Repositories Requirements 2017–2019. This page maps current policy areas to that published assessment; it does not present the historical certificate as current beyond its term.

Read the complete assessment document (PDF).

Sixteen areas of responsibility

R01

Mission and scope

Long-term access to Polish language resources and technology for research.

R02

Licences

Deposit agreements and resource licences define rights and reuse.

R03

Continuity

Institutional roles, backups and succession planning protect access.

R04

Ethics

Legal review and data-protection principles are part of appraisal.

R05

Organisation

A designated WUST team operates the service with WCSS.

R06

Expert guidance

Editors combine technical, linguistic, metadata and legal expertise.

R07

Integrity

Provenance, checksums, access control and versioning protect authenticity.

R08

Appraisal

Metadata, formats, documentation, licences and preservability are reviewed.

R09

Processes

The lifecycle documents decisions, roles and post-publication changes.

R10

Preservation plan

Preferred formats, risk monitoring and migration support future use.

R11

Data quality

Curation and depositor feedback improve resources and descriptions.

R12

Workflow

Submission, validation, editorial review, publication and care are controlled.

R13

Discovery

Persistent identifiers and public metadata support citation and harvesting.

R14

Reuse

Formats, licences, documentation and version links support proper reuse.

R15

Infrastructure

Monitored services, managed storage and backups support reliability.

R16

Security

Permissions, network controls, logging and recovery procedures reduce risk.
