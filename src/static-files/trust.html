Trust and transparency

How we maintain a trustworthy repository

CLARIN-PL combines institutional responsibility, expert curation, interoperable metadata, controlled access and long-term preservation.

Current CoreTrustSeal certification

The CLARIN-PL Language Technology Centre repository is CoreTrustSeal certified from 30 January 2026 through 29 January 2029. The assessment uses the CoreTrustSeal Requirements 2023–2025. The repository is owned by Wroclaw University of Science and Technology.

Read the complete certification document and dataset metadata.

Assessment scope

The CoreTrustSeal assessment identifies CLARIN-PL as a specialist repository serving primarily Humanities and Social Sciences researchers who use digital methods. It provides basic curation through metadata review and editing, deposit verification and post-publication monitoring.
